Remote Work FAQ

EQUIPMENT
Brightwave provides a laptop, headset and one external monitor to every employee. Additional equipment can be requested through the IT Self Service catalogue.

WORKING ABROAD
You may work from another EU country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Working from outside the EU requires approval from the People team at least four weeks in advance because of tax and insurance rules.

INTERNET COSTS
Employees who work from home at least two days per week receive a monthly internet allowance of 30 EUR, paid with the regular salary.

COWORKING SPACES
Coworking day passes are reimbursed up to 25 EUR per day for employees who live more than 50 km from a Brightwave office.
