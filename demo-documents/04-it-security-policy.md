# IT Security Policy

> Fictional document for demonstration purposes.

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused across services. Use the company password manager (1Vault) to generate and store passwords. Never share passwords, including with the IT team.

## Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the HR Portal, source control, and all cloud consoles. Use the authenticator app or a hardware security key; SMS codes are not permitted.

## Laptops and Devices

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled, automatic updates turned on, and the screen must lock after five minutes of inactivity. Personal devices may only access company email through the managed mobile app.

## Reporting Security Incidents

Report suspected phishing, lost devices, or any security incident immediately to security@brightwave.example or in the #security-incidents channel. Lost or stolen laptops must be reported within one hour so the device can be remotely wiped.

## Data Classification

Information is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Confidential and Restricted data must never be uploaded to personal cloud storage or unapproved AI tools.

## Software Installation

Only software from the approved Self Service catalogue may be installed. Requests for new software are reviewed by the IT team within three working days.
