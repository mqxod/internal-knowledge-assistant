# Sick Leave Policy

> Fictional document for demonstration purposes.

## Reporting an Absence

If you are unwell and cannot work, notify your manager before 09:30 on the first day of absence, by phone or direct message. Also record the absence in the HR Portal on the same day.

## Medical Certificates

A medical certificate (doctor's note) is required from the fourth consecutive calendar day of sickness. Upload the certificate to the HR Portal within two working days of receiving it.

## Sick Pay

Employees receive full pay for up to six weeks of sickness per illness. After six weeks, statutory sick pay rules apply. The People team will contact you to explain the next steps.

## Caring for a Sick Child

Parents may take up to ten paid days per year to care for a sick child under the age of twelve. Notify your manager as early as possible and record the absence as "Child sick leave" in the HR Portal.

## Returning to Work

After an absence of more than four weeks, your manager will offer a return-to-work conversation to agree on any adjustments, such as a gradual increase in hours.

## Mental Health

Mental health conditions are treated the same as physical illness. Employees can also access six free confidential counselling sessions per year through the Employee Assistance Programme.
