# Confidential: Executive Compensation and Restructuring Plan

> Fictional document for demonstration purposes. Upload as ADMIN-ONLY.

## Executive Bonus Pool

The executive bonus pool for the next financial year is set at 1.8 million EUR, subject to achieving 22% revenue growth. Allocation is decided by the Board Remuneration Committee in February.

## Planned Office Consolidation

The leadership team plans to close the Rotterdam office on 30 September and move the 14 affected roles to the Amsterdam office. Affected employees will be informed individually in June. This information must not be shared before the official announcement.

## Salary Bands Review

Engineering salary bands will be increased by 6% from 1 April. Bands for sales roles remain unchanged pending the commission plan review.

## Leave Buyout for Executives

Executives may sell back up to ten unused annual leave days per year at their daily base rate.
