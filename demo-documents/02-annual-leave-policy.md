# Annual Leave Policy

> Fictional document for demonstration purposes.

## Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement based on their contracted working days.

## Additional Leave for Long Service

After five full years of service, employees receive two additional days of annual leave per year. After ten years, they receive a total of four additional days.

## Requesting Leave

Leave is requested in the HR Portal. Requests of up to three days should be submitted at least one week in advance; longer absences require at least four weeks' notice. Your manager approves or declines requests within five working days.

## Carry-Over

Up to five unused leave days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be taken by 31 March, after which they expire. Exceptions require approval from the People team.

## Public Holidays

Brightwave observes the public holidays of the country in which the employee is contracted. If a public holiday falls during approved annual leave, the day is not deducted from your entitlement.

## Leave During Notice Period

Employees who resign are expected to use their remaining leave during their notice period where operationally possible. Untaken leave is paid out in the final salary payment.
