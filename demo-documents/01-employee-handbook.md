# Brightwave Employee Handbook

> Fictional document for demonstration purposes. Brightwave Technologies is not a real company.

## Welcome to Brightwave

Brightwave Technologies builds energy-monitoring software for commercial buildings. This handbook explains how we work together. Where a dedicated policy exists (for example the Annual Leave Policy or the IT Security Policy), that policy takes precedence over this summary.

## Working Hours

Our core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you may organise your working time flexibly, provided you work an average of 38 hours per week over a four-week period.

## Remote and Hybrid Work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Teams agree on at least one shared office day each week. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from your manager and the People team.

### Home Office Allowance

New employees receive a one-time home office allowance of 600 EUR for ergonomic equipment such as a chair, desk or monitor. Receipts must be submitted through the Expenses app within 60 days of purchase.

## Expenses and Travel

Business travel must be approved by your manager before booking. Economy class is standard for flights under six hours. Meal expenses during travel are reimbursed up to 45 EUR per day. Submit all expense claims within 30 days.

## Learning and Development

Every employee has an annual learning budget of 1,200 EUR for courses, books, conferences and certifications. Up to three working days per year may be used for learning activities. Requests are approved by your manager.

## Code of Conduct

We expect everyone to act with integrity, treat colleagues with respect, and speak up about concerns. Harassment and discrimination are never tolerated. Concerns can be raised with your manager, the People team, or anonymously through the Ethics Line.

## Probation Period

New employees have a probation period of six months. A probation review meeting takes place with your manager after three months and again before the end of the probation period.
